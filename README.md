# PSF_kelp HSM
Bull kelp habitat suitability in the Strait of Georgia

This study was performed in several steps of data processing indicated by the numbering on the R files
Input data: a. environmental conditions were obtained from the Salish Sea Cast model
            b. terrain features derived from bathymetry layer (20m resolution)
            c. kelp records (presence and absence) from Mora-Soto et al. (2024 a, b) - kelp presence were the centroids of mapped-kelp polygons and absences were defined as random points outside the polygons and in the bathymetric range of kelp.
